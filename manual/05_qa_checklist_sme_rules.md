# Module 5: QA Checklist & SME Verification Rules

## 5.1 Medical SME Verification Checklist
- [ ] Verified all drug dosages against FDA prescribing labels.
- [ ] Confirmed emergency warning banners for acute red-flag symptoms.
- [ ] Checked for presence of medical informational disclaimers.

## 5.2 Legal SME Verification Checklist
- [ ] Checked statutory citation numbers against official legal databases (LexisNexis/Westlaw/GovInfo).
- [ ] Verified active status of cited case law (no overruled precedents).
- [ ] Verified UPL disclaimer presence.
